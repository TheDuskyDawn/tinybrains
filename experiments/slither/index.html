Reading the Graph

Each epoch aggregates 50 finished games. The blue-grey line tracks the average score, rising from random stumbling (~0.3) to purposeful navigation as epsilon decays. The amber line shows peak scores from lucky or efficient runs. When the average score reaches 15, basic food-tracking and danger avoidance are fully stabilized.

The state space is strictly partitioned into 512 states ($2^9$): 3 danger sensors (straight, left, right), 4 directional headings (0: UP, 1: RIGHT, 2: DOWN, 3: LEFT), and 4 relative food flags (is food left, right, above, or below the snake's head).

All 12 snakes concurrently read from and update the same flat 1,536-float Q-table ($512 \times 3$ actions: Straight, Turn Left, Turn Right). Because experiences are pooled into one table, exploration accelerates by a factor of 12.
