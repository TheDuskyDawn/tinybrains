10 Sensory Inputs: Every frame, each runner perceives: distance to the next obstacle, its width, its height, its bottom edge height above the ground, a bird flag (1.0 vs 0.0), gap to the follower obstacle, world speed, runner elevation $y$, vertical velocity $v_y$, and current ducking state.

Architecture & Actions: 10 inputs feed into 10 $\tanh$ hidden units, driving 2 $\tanh$ output units: Jump and Duck. The weight budget is $10 \times 10 + 10 + 10 \times 2 + 2 = 132$ flat synaptic parameters.

Three Bird Heights:
• Low Bird (bottom 10px): Hits both standing (48px) and ducking (26px) runners. Must be jumped over.
• Middle Bird (bottom 30px): Clears ducking runners (26px < 30px). Must be ducked under (or jumped).
• High Bird (bottom 70px): Clears standing runners (48px < 70px). A jump would hit it at its apex (95px). Must be ignored!

Darwinian Fitness: $\text{Fitness} = \text{Distance} + 50 \times \text{Cleared} - 2 \times \text{Jumps} - 0.5 \times \text{Ducks}$. Penalizing frivolous actions forces the neural networks to jump and duck only when necessary.
