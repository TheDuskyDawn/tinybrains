1. Architecture & Representation

Each lander is driven by a feedforward network with 7 inputs, 10 tanh hidden neurons, and 3 outputs. Total weights: 7×10 + 10 + 10×3 + 3 = 113 parameters stored as a flat array.

Outputs are clipped to [0, 1]: Output 0 throttles the main engine along the lander's vertical axis, while Outputs 1 & 2 fire the lateral RCS thrusters to deliver precise counter-torque.

2. Neuroevolutionary Selection

Every generation preserves the top 4 elites unchanged. The remaining population is bred from the top 10 parents via single-point crossover followed by 10% Gaussian mutation.

When Shrink mutation near perfect is enabled, mutation step size scales with 0.25 × (1 − landingRate) down to a floor of 0.03, stabilizing the population as precision touchdowns proliferate.
