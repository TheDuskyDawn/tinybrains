How Hunt Works
Shared Neural Network (13 → 10 → 2, 162 weights)
Live Champion Duel
1. Predators and prey share the same 9-input network architecture, seeing only relative bearing, distance, opponent velocity, own speed, and three wall rays.
2. Every generation, agents duel across shuffled pairings plus a round against past champions in the Hall of Fame, then evolve separately from their top performers.
3. As prey learn to jink and hug walls to force sharp turns, predators are forced to stop chasing tails and start cutting corners.
