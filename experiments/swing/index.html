Reading the graph
The amber line jumps when a pendulum first discovers the swing-up trick. The blue-grey line follows as the trick spreads through crossover. A flat average far below the best usually indicates that mutation is too large or progress scaling is off.
The Underactuation Trick
Holding maximum motor torque (4.0 N·m) cannot lift a 1 kg point mass past 90° against 9.8 m/s² gravity (needs 9.8 N·m). To reach upright, networks must pump resonant kinetic energy back and forth, then brake sharply near the top into a stabilizing PD regime.

Leader Pendulum Real-time
Every individual is a feed-forward network with 4 inputs: sin(θ), cos(θ), normalized angular velocity ω/8, and previous output ut-1. The 61 parameters (40 input weights, 10 hidden biases, 10 output weights, 1 output bias) evolve without gradients. Single-point crossover recombines functional sub-circuits while adaptive Gaussian mutation refines the balance controller.
