How Neuroevolution Works

Instead of hard-coded flight rules or backpropagation with pre-labeled data, each bird is governed by a lightweight artificial neural network that evolves through Darwinian natural selection.

5 Inputs • 6 Hidden Units (tanh) • 1 Output (>0 flaps) • Live pulses from flock leader

1. Sensory Inputs

Every frame, each bird samples 5 normalized geometric variables: its altitude, vertical velocity, distance to the approaching pipe, relative height of the gap center, and gap aperture size.

2. Neural Decision

Inputs feed forward through a 6-node hidden layer with hyperbolic tangent (tanh) activation. If the output neuron exceeds 0, the bird triggers an upward flap impulse.

3. Elitism & Breeding

When all birds perish or the pipe cap is reached, birds are ranked by fitness (frames survived + pipe clearance bonuses). The top 4 elites survive verbatim; the rest are bred from the top 10 parent pool.

4. Adaptive Annealing

Synaptic weights are perturbed by Gaussian noise. As the best bird approaches the pipe cap, mutation size dynamically shrinks to fine-tune precision reflexes without corrupting mastery.
