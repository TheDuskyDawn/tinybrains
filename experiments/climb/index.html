How Climb Works

Neural Controller (49 Weights)

Architecture: 4 inputs (normalized position, velocity, hill slope, and previous throttle) pass into 8 tanh hidden units, outputting a continuous throttle force between -1 (full left) and +1 (full right).

Lines show current champion weights: amber for excitatory, blue-grey for inhibitory. Animated pulses trace live signal flow.
