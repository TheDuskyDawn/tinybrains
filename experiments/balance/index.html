Reading the graph

The amber line indicates the maximum frames survived by any cart in that generation. Upward leaps reflect stabilizing reflexes—such as timely counter-thrusts when hit by sideways pushes.

The blue-grey line shows the mean score across all 50 carts. As the average climbs toward the best line, the swarm is converging—the surviving chromosomes are dominating the parent pool.

Once a cart survives all 1,000 frames (a full 20 seconds), natural selection continues refining smooth centering and minimal angular deviation.

Neural Controller & Dynamics

Topology & Control Law (49 Weights)

Each cart is governed by a compact feedforward neural network evaluated every 0.02s physics tick:

  • 4 Normalized Inputs: Cart position ($x / 2.4$), cart velocity ($\dot{x} / 3.0$), pole angle ($\theta / 0.21$), and pole angular velocity ($\dot{\theta} / 3.0$).
  • 8 Hidden Neurons: Hyperbolic tangent ($\tanh$) non-linearities synthesizing angular momentum with track centering.
  • 1 Output ($\tanh$): Scaled by the force parameter (default 10 N) applied directly to the cart chassis.
  • Weight Matrix: $4 \times 8 + 8 \text{ (biases)} + 8 \times 1 + 1 \text{ (bias)} = 49$ total synaptic parameters.

Fairness & Non-Interacting Swarm

All 50 cart-poles share the same physical rail without colliding. Within a generation, every cart begins from identical initial conditions (a random tilt up to $\pm 0.05\text{ rad}$) and experiences the exact same periodic wind impulses (every 3 seconds). A seeded random sequence ensures fair evolutionary competition within each generation while preventing simple memorization across generations.
