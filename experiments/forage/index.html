Reading the graph

The amber line tracks the top forager's meals in each generation; the blue-grey line tracks the population average. Early generations wander until one creature discovers steering toward food. When the average climbs behind the best, the population is learning; if the average stalls far below, mutation may be too destructive.

How Forage Works

Each creature is steered by a 74-weight neural network (6 inputs, 8 hidden tanh units, 2 tanh outputs). Every generation tests all creatures on the exact same 12 cell pellet lists and starting heading, with private food so nobody can steal. The top 4 survive unchanged; the rest are bred from the top 10 with single-point crossover and Gaussian weight mutations.
